Accuracy

Tracks verified against AccurateRip or CTDB are bit-identical to rips of pressed CDs, so they are not transcodes made after the CD. This page shows how the checks behave on them: every warning or failure below is either a false alarm or a CD mastered from a lossy source. Only CD-format tracks (44.1 kHz, 16 bit) can be verified, so the hi-res and bit-depth padding checks are not covered.
